Vision Board is a place to put what you are working toward where you will actually see it — a board you build from your own photos, with journalling and guided rituals around it.

Everything lives on your device by default. Boards, entries and rituals are stored locally and encrypted at rest; you can export all of it, or delete all of it, from Settings at any time.
