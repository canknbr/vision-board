Deleting your account

Open the app, then Settings → Privacy → Delete Account. That removes your account and everything stored with it, including the photos on your device. It cannot be undone.

Exporting your data

Settings → Privacy → Export Data writes a single JSON file with every board, journal entry, gratitude list, ritual and setting, and hands it to the share sheet.

Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. Manage or cancel from the App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals and keeps access until the end of the period you already paid for. Refunds go through Apple.

Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your email. The message links to a page where you choose a new one; the link works once, for an hour.

Turning off diagnostics

Settings → Privacy → Diagnostics. Off means no crash reports leave the device. The app sends no usage analytics at all.
