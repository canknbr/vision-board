Introduction

Vision Board ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Device Data: Your boards, journal entries, rituals, and app preferences are stored on your device.
  • Account Data: When you first set up the app, we create an anonymous account for you automatically: a random user ID, with no name or email address. If you sign in with email and password or with Sign in with Apple, your email address and sign-in provider are added to that account, and a display name may be stored with it — the name you enter when you sign up, or the one Apple shares. Account data is stored by Supabase in the United States.
  • Photos: When you add photos from your photo library, they remain on your device unless you explicitly export or share them.
  • Purchases and Entitlements: If you buy or restore a subscription, purchase-related metadata may be processed to keep your entitlements in sync.
  • Stock Photos: When you use stock photos from Pexels, your search queries are sent to that service.
  • Diagnostics: If enabled, crash and error reports may be collected to help us improve app stability.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app functionality
  • To improve the app experience
  • To fix bugs and technical issues

Data Storage

Your boards, journal entries, rituals, and photos are stored on your device using local encrypted or secure storage, and are never uploaded to our servers. Your account data and subscription status are stored by Supabase, our backend provider, in the United States.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: For your account, sign-in, and subscription status. Supabase stores this data in the United States.
  • RevenueCat: For subscription purchases, entitlement management, and restore flows.
  • Pexels: For stock photos. Subject to their privacy policies.
  • Error Reporting (optional): For crash and stability diagnostics, if enabled in this build.

Your Rights

  • Export all your data at any time from Settings
  • Clear local app data at any time from Settings
  • Delete your account and account-linked data at any time from Settings
  • Control notifications, permissions, and optional account features

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app's support feature or email us.
