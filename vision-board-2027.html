How to make a 2027 vision board you will actually look at

Six steps, about half an hour, and one place to keep it.

A vision board works for a simple reason: what you see every day stays on your mind, and what stays on your mind is what you end up doing. The board does not need to be big or beautiful. It needs to be clear, and it needs to be where you look.

1. Choose a word for the year

One word for how you want 2027 to feel: calm, brave, free, home, health. It gives the board a center, and on a hard day it is the easiest part to remember.

2. Pick three to five areas of life

Career, love, health, money, travel, home, learning. More than five and the board turns into a to-do list. For each area, write one sentence in the present tense, as if it were already true: “I run a 10K in the spring.” “We live somewhere full of light.”

3. Find one image per goal

Your own photos first: a place you want to go back to, a picture from a day you felt most like yourself. Then images that feel like the goal instead of describing it. One strong image beats ten small ones.

4. Add a few affirmations

Short, in the present tense, and believable enough to say without flinching: “I finish what I start.” “I trust where I am going.” Two or three are enough.

5. Put it where you will see it

A board in a drawer does nothing. Make it your phone’s lock screen, where you will see it dozens of times a day, or print it and put it next to the mirror. Look at it for a minute every morning and read your sentences out loud.

6. Look back once a month

On the first of every month, check what moved. Mark what came true, keep the photo and the story, and change what no longer fits. A board that changes with you is one you keep looking at.
